01 Behind the Scent

The Scent of Rain: Creating Petricor

I wanted to make the exact smell of rain hitting dry earth — not a rain smell, but that specific smell. It's the scent of the world drawing a deep, cleansing breath, a moment of profound stillness before everything changes. For me, it holds a memory of elemental peace.

 02 On Emotion

Why Melancholy Smells Like Strawberries and Cream

Melancholy isn't sadness. It's the ache of something beautiful that you know you can't hold onto. For Melancolía, I needed a scent that captured that bittersweet quality — a fleeting sweetness that highlights the underlying sorrow, like a cherished, innocent memory now tinged with the knowledge of loss.

On Darkness as Luxury: The Night Collection

I didn't set out to make four dark fragrances. I set out to make four different kinds of night. Because darkness, unlike daylight, doesn't demand performance. It's where our truest, most honest selves often reside.

 04 Personal

What Vulnerability Smells Like: On Desnudo

Desnudo is the fragrance I was most afraid to make. It's about that terrifying, exhilarating moment of stripping away all armor, all ego, all pretense, to stand utterly exposed and ask to be loved. It's the thing you'd only say in the dark, whispered softly but with absolute truth.

 05 On Longing

Añoranza: The Language of Longing

Añoranza is the quiet thrum of a love that lives in memory, or across an impossible distance. It's the ache of a presence that isn't here, a phantom touch, a whispered name that still catches in your throat. This scent is built from that particular kind of longing.

 06 On Grief

Duelo: The Sacred Honor of Grief

Duelo is not about succumbing to sadness; it's about the profound, heavy honor of continuing to love someone who is no longer here. It is the brave, beautiful act of holding onto absence — allowing grief to be not a weakness, but a powerful, sacred testament to enduring love.
